About

A creative systems designer, end to end.

I am a Hardware Design Manager specializing in embedded systems, PCB development, analog/RF electronics, and connected products. I work across the complete hardware lifecycle — from system architecture and component selection to schematic design, PCB layout, prototype bring-up, testing, and design improvement.

My background combines commercial hardware development with research experience at RWTH Aachen University. My work has included analog front-end and transimpedance-amplifier design, RFID antennas, IoT biomedical systems, sensor integration, embedded control, and real-time data acquisition. I am particularly interested in developing reliable embedded and electronic products that move successfully from concept to production.

Analog/mixed-signal design

High-performance analog circuits, signal conditioning, and mixed-signal system design with focus on precision and reliability.

PCB & embedded systems

Multi-layer PCB design in Altium and firmware development for STM32, dsPIC and FPGA platforms.

FPGA bring-up & validation

Spartan, Artix and Zynq development with Verilog coding, debug and validation.

Lab data analysis & automation

Python and MATLAB-based pipelines for measurement, characterization and reporting.

AI-augmented workflow & automation

Vibe-coding with Claude, Codex and Copilot to script scrapers, automate repetitive lab and dev tasks, and compress build-test-iterate cycles.

Creative systems design
